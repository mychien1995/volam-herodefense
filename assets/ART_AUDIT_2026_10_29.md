## Visual inspection of actual references

All 16 PNGs in `assets/references/` were opened visually. The Cái Bang sheet is a concept presentation, not a valid atlas. Its target is an expressive large head with dark bangs/ponytail and red ties, compact layered red tunic, cream V collar/wraps/belt edging, dark legs/shoes and an unadorned wooden staff. The front, back and side silhouettes differ deliberately; attacks have anticipation, extension and recovery.

The hub/battle/dungeon/menu concepts show much denser authored surfaces, varied silhouettes, architectural dressing, layered foliage and ornate UI. The latest 1280×720 anti-reference shows improved viewport layout but the rejected primitive art. Environment and UI image production remain outside this character-only request.
