Use case: game VFX asset sheet. A precision sheet of isolated original contact-impact key poses, just the effect artwork, on a true transparent background. These are SMALL hit marks for a detailed 2D chibi wuxia RPG, readable downsampled to 28–48 pixels on authentic stone or grass with a stationary orthographic, slightly elevated 3/4 viewpoint. Same stylistic scale across cells. Densely painted, jagged dry-brush highlights, controlled pixel-compatible edges, clear ivory core with tapered short fragments, a sparse handful of motes. Shape readability at thumbnail scale. No heads, characters, text, letters, UI, props, weapons drawn as objects, particles across the gutters, checkerboard, scenery, giant aura rings, or black background. Only the eight small contact marks fully inside their fixed invisible cells. Each mark uses less than two thirds of the cell width and height, with completely transparent gutters. Tight tight silhouettes, one brief hit core at center, no diffuse fog, no atmospheric gradient, no large beams. Horizontal marks direction LEFT to RIGHT. Exactly 4 columns by 2 rows, landscape 1536x1024. This is Thiên Sơn, a continuous family of elegant winter wind and steel. Arctic ice white, pale cyan, cool deeper turquoise fringes. The Fan branch has wispy soft cyan folded wind ridges, the Blade branch has sharp faceted cold-steel edges. Tiny pink-white petals are reserved for Tam Liên Trảm only. The reference images establish game size and camera.
Top row, left to right: (1) Lục Dương Chưởng, two overlapping tiny rounded hooked wind strands, small and close-range for many ticks; (2) Bạo Vũ Cuồng Phong, small crescent snapped into two pale cyan splinters at the contact of a spinning fan, cut without an entire fan; (3) Bách Phiến Phong Bạo, low-density pinprick wind rosette breaking into 3 narrow slivers, compact for repeated hits, not a storm; (4) Thiên Sơn Đao basic, a single short diagonal cold-steel scoring slash, with two separated pale blue flecks.
Bottom row, left to right: (1) Phong Ảnh Hoán Vị counter hit, short clean double-step steel split and brighter central slash on counter contact, little turquoise; (2) Bách Liệt Thiên Kích contact, tightly grouped two cyan knife-edge echoes and short chips, compact to serve several blades; (3) Tam Liên Trảm first and second pass, a tiny cold-silver diagonal blade cut with two pink peach-petal fragments flicking off; (4) Tam Liên Trảm last vertical pass, compact downward ice-white fork-edged cut with three very small pale petal fragments. These are not cast trails. The 8 cels are separated and equally centered.
