Use case: game VFX asset sheet. A precision sheet of isolated original contact-impact key poses, just the effect artwork, on a true transparent background. These are SMALL hit marks for a detailed 2D chibi wuxia RPG, readable downsampled to 28–48 pixels on authentic stone or grass with a stationary orthographic, slightly elevated 3/4 viewpoint. Same stylistic scale across cells. Densely painted, jagged dry-brush highlights, controlled pixel-compatible edges, clear ivory core with tapered short fragments, a sparse handful of motes. Shape readability at thumbnail scale. No heads, characters, text, letters, UI, props, weapons drawn as objects, particles across the gutters, checkerboard, scenery, giant aura rings, or black background. Only the eight small contact marks fully inside their fixed invisible cells. Each mark uses less than two thirds of the cell width and height, with completely transparent gutters. Tight tight silhouettes, one brief hit core at center, no diffuse fog, no atmospheric gradient, no large beams. Horizontal marks direction LEFT to RIGHT. Exactly 4 columns by 2 rows, landscape 1536x1024. This is Võ Đang, using ivory, pale moon blue and charcoal indigo. Quiet taiji curvature crossing negative space, no complete emblem or glyph. Quyen branch's hits are paired ivory and ink filaments with lots of air, soft round break versus blade branch's very sharp silver-edged triangular slits. Reference 1 provides the original school's FX palettes and balance of light and dark, reference 2 shows the current game-sized scene.
Top row left to right: (1) normal bare-fist contact, a compact ivory split-comma pressure snap with a dark blue undertone, no projectile; (2) Lưỡng Nghi Thủ, single paired tiny polar folds which nearly interlock at the torso impact, one ivory and one charcoal edged with pale blue; (3) Huyền Âm Chưởng front wave first contact, cool silver-white hook with indigo ink cutouts like a torn curved paper edge, no ground ink; (4) Huyền Âm Chưởng second contact variant, a dark indigo curved snap with one bright ivory center and 2 ink fragments, clearly the same motif but deeper dark rim, not a ground detonation.
