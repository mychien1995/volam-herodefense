## Visual comparison

All eleven actual concept PNGs from Waves1–12 were opened, along with the three four-facing production review PNGs showing all twelve enemies beside Cái Bang. The actual hero idle PNG and the Wild Dog, Corrupted Monk and Stone Golem front-frame PNGs were also opened directly. An actual Phase1F arena screenshot supplied terrain/zoom context.

New designs retain elevated orthographic top-down perspective, stepped dark outlines, compact chibi proportions, warm upper-left light and limited clustered material shading. Crimson/black/purple connect the group; orange skin, bone horns, bronze armor and localized fire keep the four silhouettes distinguishable. No proprietary game character or external sprite pack was used.

The old Võ Đang reference alias is missing; the documented actual `assets/references/vo_dang_character_reference.png` was inspected, as explained in `assets/references/README.md`. The rejected placeholder screenshot was also inspected as an anti-reference.

## Validation and approval notes

All five saved PNGs were opened visually and decoded/verified. Main character poses, horns, tails, staff/axe tips and actions are fully framed. Four cardinal directions are present and correctly labeled. Final master corrections fixed reversed profiles and cultist staff handedness; the pointed crystal stays present in the casting pose.

Scale figures/rulers are **illustrative proposals**, not pixel-calibrated sprite specifications. The sheets' miniatures support silhouette review; actual arena-grass readability, frame dimensions, pivots, crowded overlaps and animation continuity remain Phase1H production checks after approval.

Please review the hound's branching ember/back-flame treatment, imp hand-fire brightness, cultist staff clearance and brute horn/axe width. Small fur/flame branches, embroidery, rivets and belt details vary slightly between the overview and enlarged boards; major costume, palette, anatomy and equipment identities remain consistent. Opaque ivory presentation is intentional; transparency was not requested.
